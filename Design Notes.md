# Aurelia Luxury Stays — Design Notes

## 1. Brand Overview

Aurelia Luxury Stays is positioned as a premium, intimate, and elevated hospitality brand for travelers seeking beautiful private accommodations, world-class experiences, and effortless luxury.

### Brand Personality
- Sophisticated
- Calm and warm
- Modern luxury
- Tailored and personal
- Exclusive but welcoming

### Core Design Direction
- Minimal, elegant layouts with generous whitespace
- Rich natural textures and warm neutrals
- High-end editorial photography
- Soft, immersive motion and transitions
- Clear conversion paths to booking and inquiries

### Visual Style
- Primary palette:
  - Warm ivory: #F7F2EA
  - Sand beige: #D7C7B4
  - Soft bronze: #A67C52
  - Charcoal black: #1E1E1E
  - Deep olive: #364A3A
- Typography:
  - Headings: Cormorant Garamond or high-contrast serif
  - Body/UI: Manrope, Inter, or a modern geometric sans-serif
- Imagery style:
  - Natural light
  - Lush environment shots
  - Interior lifestyle photography
  - Wide cinematic framing

---

## 2. Global UI Notes

### Layout System
- Full-bleed hero sections on top-level landing pages
- Strong content rhythm using large whitespace and modular cards
- Elegant grid-based layouts for properties and editorial content
- Sticky booking or inquiry modules on detail pages

### Buttons and CTAs
- Primary CTA: "Book Your Stay"
- Secondary CTA: "Explore Villas", "View Details", "Plan My Experience"
- Buttons use warm bronze or charcoal with subtle hover elevation
- Rounded corners but refined and minimal; avoid overly playful shapes

### Cards
- Polished image cards with soft shadow, subtle border, and consistent spacing
- Titles use elegant serif styling for premium feel
- Hover states should feel refined: slight upward motion, image zoom, soft shadow increase

### Navigation
- Simple luxury navigation: Home, Stays, Experiences, Journal, About, Contact
- Top navigation should feel airy and premium with a sticky transparent-to-solid effect on scroll
- Mobile menu should collapse into a minimal, elegant drawer with clear hierarchy

---

## 3. Page-by-Page Design Notes

## Page 1: Home / Landing Page

### Purpose
Introduce the brand, showcase the most desirable stays, and lead visitors to book.

### Hero Section
- Full-viewport hero image of luxury villa or resort exterior
- Strong headline: "Stay Somewhere Extraordinary"
- Supporting copy: short description of the collection and service
- Primary button: "Explore Stays"
- Secondary button: "Plan My Trip"
- Optional luxury trust line: "Private villas • Curated concierge • 24/7 care"

### Content Sections
- Featured properties: 3–4 stay cards with image, location, price, and short summary
- Signature experiences: curated activities like private dining, wellness retreats, boat charters
- Brand promise: intimate service, design-led properties, unforgettable moments
- Customer testimonials: elegant quote layout with guest names and destination
- Destination highlights: select regions to appeal to travelers seeking luxury escapes

### Design Notes
- Hero should feel immersive and cinematic
- Visual hierarchy must keep CTA above the fold
- Use warm neutral palette and highly curated photography
- Keep copy luxurious but concise

---

## Page 2: Stays / Collection Page

### Purpose
Display all available properties in a clean, browsable catalog.

### Layout
- Large heading: "The Collection"
- Filter toolbar for: destination, guests, budget, amenities, property type
- Property grid with 6–12 cards depending on screen size
- Each card includes image, location, name, nightly rate, amenities, and rating

### Card Structure
- Large top image
- Title and subtitle below
- Feature tags like: Pool, Ocean View, 4 Bedrooms, Concierge
- CTA: "View Details"

### Filters
- Minimal glass or soft white filter panel
- Clear labels and dropdowns
- Keep filter interactions smooth and intuitive

### Design Notes
- This page should feel editorial and premium, not overly commercial
- Prioritize high-quality photography and balanced spacing
- Use a clean product-grid system that remains elegant on mobile

---

## Page 3: Property Detail Page

### Purpose
Help guests evaluate a specific villa and complete booking decisions.

### Top Layout
- Full-width gallery carousel with multiple villa images
- Sticky booking summary card on the right side on desktop
- Quick property overview: location, guests, bedrooms, price, rating

### Sections
- Overview / introduction
- Features and amenities list
- Included services and concierge benefits
- Guest reviews
- Suggested experiences nearby
- Booking availability and date selection
- Policies and terms

### Booking Card
- Fixed summary panel with date selection, guest count, nightly rate, cleaning fees, deposit details
- CTA: "Reserve This Stay"
- Include secure trust text and service highlights

### Design Notes
- Content should feel premium and highly informative
- Use a timeline of amenities and benefits rather than dense text blocks
- Bookings should feel easy, reassuring, and clear

---

## Page 4: Experiences Page

### Purpose
Show what guests can do during their stay beyond accommodation.

### Layout
- Hero with immersive travel imagery
- Experience categories: private dining, wellness, water activities, excursions, celebrations
- Cards with image, title, overview, duration, and pricing

### Experience Card Structure
- Large image or short video thumbnail
- Concise title and short description
- Optional tags such as "Private", "Curated", "5-Star"
- CTA: "Plan This Experience"

### Design Notes
- Feels aspirational but not cluttered
- Add enough value to suggest exclusivity and personalized service
- Use warm editorial layouts and soft contrast between sections

---

## Page 5: Dining & Wellness Page

### Purpose
Highlight culinary and wellness offerings that enhance the luxury stay experience.

### Layout
- Split hero or two-column content blocks
- Culinary section with private chefs, wine pairings, in-villa dining, destination dining
- Wellness section with spa treatments, yoga, recovery, fitness, and personalized wellness plans

### Content Style
- Focus on rituals, comfort, and personalization
- Include service descriptions, ambiance notes, and sample itineraries

### Design Notes
- Use softer backgrounds and more atmospheric imagery
- Emphasize the sense of slow luxury and individualized care

---

## Page 6: Journal / Stories Page

### Purpose
Build emotional storytelling, increase brand depth, and reinforce destination appeal.

### Layout
- Editorial landing page with featured story hero
- Grid of article cards below
- Categories such as destination guides, villa features, travel advice, personal stories

### Cards
- Large feature image
- Title and short excerpt
- Date and category label
- CTA: "Read More"

### Design Notes
- Content should feel magazine-like and elevated
- Use asymmetrical or relaxed editorial grid for more modern luxury feel
- Maintain consistent image treatment and readable typography

---

## Page 7: About / Brand Story Page

### Purpose
Communicate the brand’s identity, ethos, and the story behind the experience.

### Sections
- Mission statement
- How Aurelia began
- Founder or curator note
- Why each stay is selected
- Values: privacy, comfort, design, care, originality
- Sustainability and responsibility focus

### Visual Approach
- Use editorial storytelling with soft, human-centered imagery
- Include a timeline or a few quote blocks rather than heavy text blocks

### Design Notes
- This page should feel personal and authentic
- Avoid corporate language; use warm, elevated phrasing that feels refined

---

## Page 8: Booking Page

### Purpose
Guide travelers to confirm reservation details with clarity and confidence.

### Flow
1. Select destination and dates
2. Choose property or package
3. Add guests and services
4. Review pricing and policies
5. Enter guest details
6. Payment and confirmation

### Layout
- Multi-step progress bar or numbered steps
- Left column: booking form and details
- Right column: reservation summary card

### Form Design
- Large, easy-to-read fields
- Clear labels and spacing
- Optional add-ons: airport transfer, chef, spa, wine service

### Design Notes
- High trust and clarity are essential
- Keep forms minimal and not overwhelming
- Use subtle confirmation states and reassuring microcopy

---

## Page 9: Contact Page

### Purpose
Provide a direct communication channel for inquiries, concierge requests, and custom travel planning.

### Layout
- Intro copy with a premium concierge feel
- Contact form with fields such as name, email, trip dates, destination, message
- Contact information, phone, email, and general support line
- Optional location details or office map

### Design Notes
- Minimal but polished
- Strong CTA: "Speak with a concierge"
- Structured and easy to process on mobile

---

## Page 10: FAQ Page

### Purpose
Answer common trip planning and property questions before guests contact support.

### Layout
- Accordion sections with categories like stays, booking, arrival, policies, amenities, concierge
- Search or quick links for common topics

### Design Notes
- Keep content concise and warm
- Focus on clarity, usability, and trust-building

---

## Page 11: Confirmation / Thanks Page

### Purpose
Reinforce confidence after booking and provide next steps.

### Content
- Booking reference number
- Confirmation summary
- Arrival details and what to expect next
- Concierge support contact
- Additional helpful trip guidance

### Design Notes
- Calm, reassuring visual tone
- Include a clear next-step path: itinerary, destination details, and support contact

---

## 12. Shared Footer

### Content
- Brand statement
- Navigation links
- Social links
- Contact details
- Newsletter signup (optional)
- Copyright and legal links

### Design Notes
- Footer should feel premium but not heavy
- Maintain strong spacing and subtle typography hierarchy
- Use concise and elegant copy

---

## 13. UX & Interaction Principles

- Keep experiences polished and slow rather than flashy
- Use subtle micro-animations on hover, image transitions, and scroll reveals
- Maintain clear product hierarchy and visual rhythm
- Ensure accessibility: accessible contrast, focus states, readable text sizes, and keyboard-friendly interactions
- Design for mobile-first responsive behavior while preserving luxury quality

---

## 14. Conversion Strategy

### Primary Goal
Drive bookings and concierge inquiries while maintaining a luxurious, confidence-building experience.

### Conversion Best Practices
- Strong top-of-page CTA on landing and property pages
- Value-focused copy that speaks to exclusivity and service
- Trust signals: reviews, service details, privacy, and secure payment reassurance
- Personal, concierge-driven language instead of aggressive sales language

---

## 15. Final Visual Summary

This site should feel like an elevated design magazine meets a trusted luxury hospitality brand. Every page should communicate calm confidence, impeccable taste, and a personalized travel experience. The visual language should remain minimal and premium, using texture, warm neutrals, and curated imagery to create a quiet sense of luxury that invites trust and desire.

Aurelia Luxury Stays should feel less like a hotel booking engine and more like an intimate, design-led travel concierge experience.
